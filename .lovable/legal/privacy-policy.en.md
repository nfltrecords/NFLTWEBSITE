# NFLT RECORDS — Draft Privacy Policy

**Private, unpublished and unapproved working version. No effective date. Proposed wording and unresolved decisions are separated so assumptions are not represented as facts. At the owner's request, the complete text is readable in the development preview only; it is not a final policy approved for publication.**

## 1. Scope and contact

This draft concerns the NFLT RECORDS website, its contact and licensing/availability inquiry forms, and its technical operation. It does not describe orders, payments or customer downloads as existing: these services are not open on the site. It also does not describe processing specific to Spotify or Google Play, reached through external links.

SAS NFLT RECORDS FRANCE is associated with physical CD purchases under the owner's clarification, without being the default seller for digital downloads, licensing or all online purchases. Registration details and contact information must be verified before publication. Its precise role as website data controller, and any roles of NFLT RECORDS INC. or other entities, must be confirmed before publication. No DPO or telephone number is announced.

## 2. Information submitted through forms

The contact form collects name, email and message. Validation requires a non-empty name of up to 100 characters, a valid email of up to 255 characters, and a non-empty message of up to 2000 characters. All three are necessary for saving the inquiry. Contact from the English version leads to the French form.

Licensing and availability inquiries collect name, email, organization/project, inquiry type, optional release/UPC, intended use, territory, duration, message and language. Name, email and message are necessary (limits of 100, 255 and 3000 characters respectively). Organization/project, release selection, territory and duration may be left blank. Type, use and language are also saved: type and use have default values, with language set by the FR/EN version. Organization, territory and duration each have a 150-character limit. A default use may be saved even for an availability inquiry, where that field is hidden. The system adds an intended recipient and recording metadata according to the table; the complete metadata inventory requires confirmation.

Visitors are not asked to submit banking or sensitive data. Free-text fields can contain additional information chosen by the sender; minimization must be organized.

## 3. Current uses and legal bases

The forms receive and save inquiries addressed to the label. Error reporting supports technical diagnosis. Audience measurement described in section 5 provides information about site traffic.

No final legal basis has been confirmed for these processing operations. Purpose-specific assessments (correspondence, precontractual inquiries, security, audience measurement and possible evidential retention) are retained in the internal checklist. This document does not represent consent as collected or legitimate interests as already assessed. Publishing a final policy requires prior validation.

No newsletter form, advertising pixel or payment integration was identified in the inspected code. This limited observation does not establish the absence of all processing on the published site. This draft provides for no additional commercial prospecting.

## 4. Recipients and providers

Inquiries are saved in Lovable Cloud. Handling by authorized label personnel must be defined and documented. Saving data is not sending an email: inspected functions trigger no notification. Data is not represented as automatically transferred to NFLT RECORDS INC. or a licensing partner.

Identified services are Lovable for the platform, hosting and error reporting, Lovable Cloud for inquiry storage, and Google for external fonts. Exact contractual legal entities, GDPR roles, subprocessors, contracts and operational access remain to be confirmed. This text does not claim an exhaustive approved recipient list.

## 5. Lovable audience measurement — proposed paragraph to retain

The inspected project settings show Visitor analytics and Click tracking enabled. With no banner passing a choice to the service in the inspected code, clicks and the information below are recorded for every visitor. Page visits, time on page, loading speed and errors are also recorded for every visitor, without cookies and without identifying people.

Lovable’s audience measurement uses no cookies, stores nothing on visitors’ devices, does not retain IP addresses and cannot recognize returning visitors. It records pages visited, where visits came from, country, browser, operating system and device type, time spent, loading speed, errors, and clicks on buttons and links. Inspected code sends exceptions but no additional custom business events. Detailed data is retained for 90 days and daily summaries for one year. Lovable collects this data for us. [Lovable’s privacy policy](https://lovable.dev/privacy) explains how it handles it.

These characteristics concern this measurement service only, not forms, all hosting or other site services. Configuration choices and the legal basis must be validated before publishing the final policy.

## 6. Technical data, fonts and error reporting

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts: browsers contact fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. These requests communicate connection data to the provider; actual headers, use, retention and locations require verification. No absence of transfers outside the EEA is inferred from this use.

Code provides for reporting exceptions and context, including the page path, message and potentially an error stack, to Lovable. Server logs may also contain diagnostic information. Their exact content, filtering, recipients, location and retention are unvalidated. No test deliberately causes a leak or an error containing form data.

## 7. Cookies and browser storage

Fresh-browser observation without sign-in or form submission detected cookies named `__cf_bm` and `__dpl` on the published site and one session-storage entry after visiting forms. It does not establish their purposes, providers, durations or legal exemptions; these must be documented from configurations and contracts. No cookie was observed on the same local journey, which does not describe complete production behavior.

The authentication client provides browser-storage session persistence if authentication is used. This does not mean a session was created for the observed anonymous visitors. The inventory must cover relevant authenticated journeys, form submissions, redirects and external services. No general “no cookies” or “no trackers” assertion is authorized. No CMP or consent transmission was identified in the inspected journey; applicable rules and any necessary changes remain to be assessed.

## 8. International transfers

Locations for inquiry storage, backups, logs and provider access are unconfirmed. Transfers outside the EEA, affected countries and safeguards (for example an applicable adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses with necessary assessments) must be established from evidence for each processing operation. Neither an absence of transfers nor the existence of a particular contractual safeguard is asserted here.

## 9. Retention periods

The only documented retention periods in this audit concern Lovable audience measurement, set out in section 5. No retention period or effective deletion was validated for contact messages, licensing/availability inquiries, technical logs, sessions or backups. Active periods, any archiving, retention-end criteria, access and deletion procedures must be defined by purpose with legal justification. No automatic purge is announced.

## 10. Individuals’ rights

Depending on processing and its legal basis, individuals have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and, where its conditions are met, portability. Where processing relies on consent, it may be withdrawn without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Applicable legal limits and exceptions must be considered.

The proposed rights contact is [coordonnées à confirmer avant publication]; its ability to receive and handle requests must be confirmed. Proportionate identity evidence may be requested where reasonable doubts exist, without excessive systematic collection. The process must meet GDPR time limits (normally one month, with a reasoned extension of two months where permitted), without claiming it is already operational. A complaint may be submitted to the CNIL and, depending on circumstances, the competent supervisory authority.

## 11. Security and document changes

Observed production pages use HTTPS; code validates and limits inquiry fields. These points are neither security certification nor proof of comprehensive compliance. Permissions, data safeguards, contracts, backups, incident handling and minimization rules still require verification. No unverified encryption-at-rest or security mechanism is announced.

Publication requires resolving open issues and approval by the data controller. Processing related to possible future sales requires an update before activation. No effective date or overall legal compliance is declared.
