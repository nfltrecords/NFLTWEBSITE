## 7. Withdrawal and digital content

The French provisions in this section are a preparatory module to use only after reviewing applicability to the contract and consumer concerned, including where INC. is seller. They do not establish the rules for all worldwide sales. Under the applicable French framework, consumers generally have fourteen days to withdraw from a distance contract; for digital content not supplied on a tangible medium, the period runs from contract conclusion. A simple availability inquiry is not an order and does not start this contractual period.

The exception for digital content not supplied on a tangible medium cannot be presumed. For a contract imposing an obligation to pay, supply started before the period expires can cause the right to be lost only when statutory conditions are satisfied: prior express consent to early performance, acknowledgment of loss of the right and confirmation of the agreement on a durable medium. Payment alone, submitting a form or a pre-ticked checkbox must not be treated as satisfying these conditions. This exception does not remove statutory guarantees.

Withdrawal arrangements for each product category, seller and territory remain subject to legal review before any sale. No withdrawal mechanism is represented as already operational. References and functions still requiring verification are retained in the internal checklist, not announced as established obligations for every transaction.
