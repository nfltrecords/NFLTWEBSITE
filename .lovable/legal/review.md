## Internal terms architecture comparison — not an approved sales design

- Separate entity/territory terms: clearer accountability and jurisdiction-specific tax/consumer notices, but duplicate drafting, translation and version maintenance can diverge. Requires explicit routing to the correct seller/territory and retention of the accepted version; still cannot waive mandatory protections by document selection.
- Modular terms: shared non-transactional/product principles plus an explicit seller-of-record identification schedule and legally reviewed entity/territory annexes reduces duplication. Risk: conflicting modules or an ambiguous seller if rules/version precedence are not explicit. The complete applicable package must be readable before commitment and confirmed durably, not assembled invisibly or left for the customer to guess.
- Preparatory approach only: keep the FR/EN documents as a common draft with separate physical-CD, digital-download and licensing modules, plus expressly conditional French withdrawal/mediation provisions. Obtain counsel approval of the structure for each validated seller/product/territory combination; switch to separate terms where necessary. Do not generate a public country menu, assign sellers by geography or invent a governing law now.
- Required internal approval matrix: product category (physical CD, digital download or licensing), product/UPC and controlled rights; actual contracting entity and fulfillment role; customer territory and directed markets; validated seller particulars/contact; tax/total-price treatment; governing-law analysis and non-waivable consumer rules (information, conformity, withdrawal, mediation/remedies); complete terms language/version and durable confirmation. Mixed-seller orders, if ever envisaged, require separately identified contracts or a legally approved arrangement; no single seller is assumed for them.
- French Code references in the existing drafts are research for applicable configurations only, not a global choice of French law. INC. records remain separate; no worldwide rights, territorial sales permission or new tax regime inferred from the internal INC.→FRANCE authorization, which is not a default seller allocation. Private configuration contains only requirements and empty assignment lists; checkout stays false.
